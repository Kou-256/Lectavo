FFmpeg and PyAV
---------------

Lectavo does not distribute or invoke a standalone ffmpeg.exe. Video and audio
decoding use PyAV and OpenCV wheels that include FFmpeg libraries. FFmpeg is a
separate project and is not owned by Lectavo. Before publishing a binary
release, the distributor must provide corresponding source and build
information as required by the applicable licenses.

Whisper models
--------------

Whisper model weights are not included in Lectavo installers. They are obtained
on first transcription use and cached under the current user's local app data.
Each model remains subject to its upstream license and terms.
